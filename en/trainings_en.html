Safety Trainings

Georgian legislation requires that safety training and briefings be conducted for employees and 3rd party representatives in the workplace. We can offer you:

  • Emergency simulation and evacuation, during which company employees and managers learn how to properly evacuate from a hazardous area, assemble, account for personnel, and participate in search and rescue operations. The simulation also measures the time required for evacuation—a factor of critical importance for saving lives during an actual emergency.
  • The practical application of pipeline and instrument diagrams for the isolation of hazardous energy, as well as the maintenance and recording of isolation logs.
