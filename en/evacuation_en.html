Evacuation Maps and Plans

To ensure the safe evacuation of people from buildings, educational institutions, indoor gathering places, and workspaces during an emergency, a properly prepared evacuation plan and map—compliant with established standards—are required.

An evacuation map is also vital for the fire fighting brigade during search and rescue operations, to navigate properly in enclosed areas

An evacuation plan is required for workspaces and public gathering places where more than 10 people congregate on a single floor. Listed below are the Georgian and international laws and standards that mandate the presence of an evacuation map:

  • Law of Georgia on Occupational Safety (4283-IIს)
  • Technical Regulation on Fire Safety Rules and Conditions (No: 370)
  • ISO 23601
  • NFPA 101

A properly prepared evacuation map must clearly and distinctly display information such as:

  • Primary and alternative routes to evacuation exits
  • A building plan (floor plan) showing stairs, walls, and windows.
  • Pictograms (icons) indicating the location of material resources required for emergency response (fire extinguishers, first aid supplies, etc.)
  • Pictograms and safety signs for various safety and evacuation processes, prepared in accordance with the ISO 7010 standard (for international recognizability)
  • Various technical details, such as: address, building floor, specific location, room number, etc.

The evacuation map must be of an appropriate size and placed in a prominent area, in a location clearly visible to employees and visitors.
