Pipeline and Instrumentation Diagram (P&ID)

Piping and instrumentation diagrams (P&ID)are essential and vital for safe operation of oil and gas, chemical, hydraulic and automated fire fighting equipment processes.

In oil and gas sector and chemical plants, P&ID's are required by following Georgian and European legislaitons:

  • Law of Georgia on Occupational Safety
  • Technical Regulation About Safe Operation of Oil Depots

Eur-Lex SAVEO III Directive (European Union)

P&ID's contain following items/ojbects:

  • Vessels (Storage tanks, pressure vessels, separators)
  • Equipment, such as pumps, compressors, filters
  • მPipelines
  • Valves (Manual and automated)
  • Mechanical Gauges for temperature, flow and pressure measurements
  • Electrical instrumentations for transmitters and connection diagrams for each one

What is the purpose ant utilization of P&ID's?

  1. Safe operation and control
  2. Preparation of plant or equipment for maintenance, which includes isolation of flow streams and draining/venting (LO/TO)
  3. FOr applying modifications and Management of Change/MOC
  4. Process hazard analysis (HAZOP, HAZID)
  5. Emergency response

Scope of application

Thse systems utilize P&ID's:

  • Fire suppression systems (Manual or Automted)
  • Gas and refuelling stations
  • Oil depots and tank yards
  • Agricultural irrigation systems
  • Sewage systems
  • Chemical plants producing air, liquid or solid substances
