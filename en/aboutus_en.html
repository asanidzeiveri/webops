Our goal, Skills and Experience

We believe that knowledge, diligence, and a positive attitude will enable us to make workspaces and public gathering places safer.

The DRAWDRAFT Solutions team consists of specialists with the expertise required to offer clients the best product on the market. Here is a brief overview of the education and skills of our team members:

  • Public relations and costumer service
  • Product quality control and sales management
  • Certified Safety Professional
  • Fire safety and building evacuation (ILO)
  • Chemical process safety and engineering
  • Hazardous waste management and emergency response (HAZWOPER)
  • Process automatization and electrical safety systems (SCADA)

  • South Caucasus Pipeline Gas Compressor Station Operations in 2023-2026
